# Arctic Prime

## Role

Arctic Prime is the laptop/control hub.

## Responsibilities

- Secondary KeePassXC synced device.
- Ford Link `D:` usually stays with Arctic Prime.
- Keeps a secondary local vault/device copy for offline access.

## Known Issue

- Wi-Fi or Bluetooth Code 10 may happen unpredictably.
- This is likely a driver, wake, or chip power-state issue.

## Sync Notes

- The local vault still works offline.
- Network sync may be unreliable.
- If the network is unstable, avoid editing both Control Prime and Arctic Prime before a merge.
- Do not store secrets in this repository.
